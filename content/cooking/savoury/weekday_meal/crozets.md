This was my favorite dish as a child, oozing with melted butter, cheese, and finely sliced ham.
It now lives in a very different, cheeseless (!) iteration, when I can get the pasta...

## Ingredients (single serving):

* 80 grams of [crozets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crozets_de_Savoie)
* salt
* a few cracks of black pepper
* red chili flakes to taste
* 1/6th of an onion, finely diced[^onion]
* 1 spicy sausage, diced[^meat]
* 1 tablespoon(ish) of butter, finely diced

[^onion]: I used to do this with scallions or chives. But I always have onions in my pantry and, finely diced, they do just as good a job if not better.
[^meat]: Many meats work here. I long used uncooked vegan sausages, diced ham, diced turkey ham, etc.

## Preparation:

* put the crozets, salt, and just enough water to cover them in a pan
* cook until done, usually about 15 minutes, by which point the water should have boiled down into a thick starchy sauce[^water]
* meanwhile, roast the diced sausages in the oven, flipping them from time to time
* add the onion, pepper, and chili flakes
* mix on high heat until you reach your desired consistency
* add the sausages, then, off the heat, the diced butter
* mix vigorously until the butter has fully dissolved

[^water]: If you lack water, you can always pour a little in as you go. If there is too much, the crozets can survive some cooking and vigorous stirring on high heat as you evaporate it to your prefered consistency.
